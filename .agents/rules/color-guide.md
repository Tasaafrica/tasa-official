## Tasa Africa — Colour Usage Rules

Use the following brand colours consistently across the entire application:

- **Slate `#334155`**: Primary text, headings, navigation, footer, and primary buttons.
- **Ocean Blue `#489FB5`**: Main brand accent, links, secondary buttons, and interactive elements.
- **Sky Blue `#82C0CC`**: Subtle accents, illustrations, decorative elements, and selected states.
- **Warm Neutral `#EDE7E3`**: Soft section backgrounds and occasional panels.
- **Amber Orange `#FFA62B`**: Sparingly used for highlights, badges, and important emphasis.

### Usage Guidelines
1. Keep white and near-white backgrounds dominant to maintain a clean, comfortable, professional interface.
2. Use slate for strong visual structure and readable text.
3. Use ocean blue as the primary accent; do not give every brand colour equal prominence.
4. Use sky blue and amber sparingly. Avoid large, competing areas of accent colours.
5. Maintain consistent colour usage across all pages, components, dashboards, and responsive layouts.
6. Ensure accessible text contrast. Do not use light colours for small text on light backgrounds.
7. Use semantic colours for success, warning, error, and informational states independently of the brand palette.
8. Reuse existing CSS variables or design tokens. Do not introduce new colour values or hardcode arbitrary colours when an existing token is appropriate.
9. Do not introduce gradients, coloured shadows, or decorative colour effects unless they serve a clear design purpose.
10. Remove legacy teal `#0F766E` and mint `#CCFBF1` from the active brand palette.

**Core principle:** Tasa Africa must feel professional, modern, comfortable, and community-oriented. Every colour must serve a clear functional or visual purpose.
